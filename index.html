NLP techniques used

Text cleaning and tokenization

Removes URLs and non-letter characters, lowercases tweet text, then splits it into words.

Word and character TF-IDF

Combines word unigrams and bigrams with character n-grams to capture useful terms and word patterns.

Logistic Regression

Uses the TF-IDF features to estimate whether a tweet is disaster-related or non-disaster.
